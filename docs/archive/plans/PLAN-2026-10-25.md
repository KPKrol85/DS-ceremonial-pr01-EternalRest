## Optional improvements

- [x] **O-01 — Responsive hero images** — Added 12 AVIF/WebP variants for three heroes with `srcset`/`sizes` and unchanged originals; deterministic generation, build, reference checks and 54 Chromium scenarios passed; variants were 29.4–85.5% smaller, but LCP was not measured.
- [x] **O-02 — Extend Playwright smoke coverage** — Added theme and pricing-details tests and menu resize coverage, expanding the suite from 10 to 12 tests; focused tests passed 3/3 and the full Chromium suite passed 12/12.
- [x] **O-03 — Migrate ESLint to Flat Config** — Replaced `.eslintrc.cjs` with `eslint.config.js`, explicitly declared `@eslint/js`/`globals` and retained ESLint 8.57.1, rules and scope; `npm ci`, lint, formatting and configuration comparisons passed on Windows without diagnostic changes; Linux and Playwright were not retested for this task.
